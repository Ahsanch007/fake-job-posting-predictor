TF-IDF = Term Frequency × Inverse Document Frequency

TF-IDF ke 2 parts

TF = Term Frequency

Word ek particular job mein kitni baar appear ho raha hai.

IDF = Inverse Document Frequency

Word poore dataset mein kitna common ya uncommon hai.

Simple idea:

common word → less importance
distinctive word → more importance

Isliye TF-IDF model ko job posting ke important words ke patterns identify karne mein help karta hai.


simple idea

Common word
    ↓
Low importance

Rare but useful word
    ↓
Higher importance


TF-IDF ki zarurat kyun hai?

Agar hum sirf word count use karein, to common words bhi important appear ho sakte hain.

TF-IDF common words ki importance reduce karta hai aur relatively distinctive words ko zyada weight deta hai.

TF-IDF = Text ko numbers mein convert karta hai aur words ki importance calculate karta hai.

3. Precision

Model ne jitni jobs ko Fake bola, un mein se kitni actually Fake thin.

4. Recall

Jo jobs actually Fake thin, un mein se model ne kitni Fake detect kar li.

5. F1-score

Precision aur Recall ka combined measure.

Ek line mein yaad rakho

Precision = Jab model Fake bole, kitna sahi hai?
Recall = Jo Fake actually hain, un mein se kitne pakre?
F1 = Precision + Recall ka balance
